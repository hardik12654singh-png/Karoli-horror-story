WELCOME TO KAROLI

Vo dono KAROLI pahuch to gaye aur wahe pe sashi kant naam ka ek ajeeb chote kad ka Admi miila jo dil se Acha tha jo unhe apne ghar do raat rukne ki ijaajat de deta hai. raat ko khaate samay unko sashi kant batata ki Karoli ek aisa gaav hai jaha raat bhi normal nhi hoti.Sath hi usne St. jude's naam ke church se bhi unhe warn kiya ki "ek Haunted church hai" par vo tohj aaye hi haunted jagah keliye the.

Gaon walon ne warning di thi… “Raat ke baad church ke paas mat jaana… wahan awaaz aati hai.”

Obviously… humne ignore kar diya.

Dare simple tha… 12 baje raat ko abandoned St. Jude’s Church jao… aur altar par candle jalao.

Sharad ne dheere se bola, “Bhai… ye sab movies me thik lagta hai… real life me nahi…”

Hardik ne halka sa smirk kiya, “Relax… bas 5 minute ka kaam hai… ghost bhi so raha hoga.”

Church ke gate ke paas pahuchte hi hawa thandi ho gayi. Bilkul ajeeb tarike se.

“Notice kiya?” Sharad ne fuskara. “Abhi tak garmi thi… yahan thand kyun hai?”

Hardik chup ho gaya.

CREEEEEEAAAAKKKK…

Gate apne aap khul gaya.

“Main andar nahi jaa raha…” Sharad ne seedha bol diya.

“Ab aaya hai toh jaana padega,” Hardik ne candle tight pakdi.

Andar andhera itna gehra tha ki torch ki roshni bhi kam lag rahi thi.

Smell… mitti… purani wax… aur kuch jo samajh nahi aa raha tha…

Suddenly… bench par koi baitha tha.

Ek NUN.

Bilkul seedhi. Bilkul chup.

“Hardik… woh insaan hai ya…?” Sharad ki awaaz hil rahi thi.

“Mujhe nahi pata… par woh hilli toh hai…”

CRACK… CRACK…

Nun dheere dheere khadi hui. Jaise uske joints toot rahe ho.

“BHAI NAHI…” Hardik peeche hata.

Nun ka sir dheere dheere ghoom gaya. Pura 180 degree.

Aankhein… poori kaali.

“Tum… yahan… kyun aaye ho…” usne ghiste hue awaaz me bola.

Sharad cheekha, “MUMMYYYYY!!!”

Dono bhaag pade.

Bahar aaye… par kuch bhi nahi tha.

No bike. No car.

Sirf… cycle.

“Chori karni padegi,” Hardik ne seedha bola.

“Aaj ke baad main sudhar jaunga,” Sharad bola.

Suddenly…

“BHAI WOH UDD RAHI HAI!!!”

Nun hawa me thi. Seedha unke peeche.

“PEDAL MAAR!!!” Hardik chillaaya.

Cycle hil rahi thi… road uneven tha… aur peeche maut udd rahi thi.

Bridge cross karte hi…

Sab ruk gaya.

Nun static ho gayi.

Phir dheere dheere… dhuaan ban gayi.

Dono ghar pahunch ke seedha gir gaye.

Sharad hasa, “Humne bhoot ke saath race kari…”

Hardik bola, “Aur cycle bhi chori ki…”

Dono phir hasne lage…

-By Hardik Singh and Sharad Raj Purohit
